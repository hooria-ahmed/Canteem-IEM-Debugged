Frequently Asked Questions
How are ingredient costs tracked?
Ingredient costs are managed in the Inventory -> Update Cost tab. The system automatically recalculates the overall Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) for your dishes based on their recipes whenever ingredient prices change.
What happens when an item goes out of stock?
If an item's raw materials fall below required levels, it will be flagged as "Low Stock" or "Out of Stock" on the POS Terminal to prevent cashiers from selling items the kitchen cannot make.
How do I add a new expense?
Navigate to the Finance Hub and click "Log New Expense". Make sure to choose the correct date, category, and descriptive name so your Profit & Loss statements remain accurate.
